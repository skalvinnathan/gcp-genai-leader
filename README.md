# Google Cloud Generative AI Leader — Study Hub

A self-contained study site for the Google Cloud **Generative AI Leader** certification, written in very simple English for learners who want every idea explained from zero (short sentences, every term defined, daily-life pictures, worked examples).

Facts were checked against Google's own pages on **15 November 2026** (see "Verified facts" below).

## Contents

- **`index.html`**: the study hub (one file, inline CSS and JS, no build step, no dependencies, no network needed).
  - Home with saved progress (quiz, flashcards, pages visited, study plan)
  - **How the exam works**: facts, the four domains and weights, a plain-words checklist of every exam-guide section
  - **The learning path**: the 5 courses of path 1951 and what each one covers (own words)
  - One page per domain: Fundamentals (~30%), Google Cloud offerings (~35%), Improving output (~20%), Business strategy (~15%)
  - **Names in 2026**: what Google renamed (Vertex AI to Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Vertex AI Studio to Agent Studio, and more)
  - Keyword to service catalog, comparison tables, **traps** (confusing pairs), numbers to remember
  - **Glossary** (128 terms, searchable), **30 flashcards**, **2-week study plan**, exam-day tips
  - **Practice quiz**: 115 original scenario questions, split across domains in proportion to the exam weights (34 / 39 / 24 / 18), tagged by domain and difficulty. Each has the right answer, why it is right, and why every wrong option is wrong. Filters (domain, level, unanswered, wrong, flagged), shuffle, results by domain, keyboard shortcuts, progress saved in the browser.
  - Light and dark theme (follows your system, or switch by hand), works on phones, keyboard friendly
- **`cheatsheet.html`**: one page keyword to service quick reference, printable.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser (double click). Everything is inline.

**GitHub Pages:** repo Settings, Pages, deploy from `main` / root. The site will be at <https://skalvinnathan.github.io/gcp-genai-leader/>.
